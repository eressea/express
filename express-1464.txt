== Konvergenz ==

=== World 28 ===
==== Four weeks ago ====

The fight against the Blight offered Te Atairangikaahu neither respite nor restful sleep. Dispatches
arrived in relentless succession, and the Blight kept eating away at everything it touched.
The conflict would leave deep scars—that much was already certain.
She studied the latest enemy positions.

That was when the pendant manifested.
First came a sharp, powerful tug against the nape of her neck. Then it grew intensely hot, flooding 
the room with a glowing red light.
She grasped her Birth Seashell hanging from the end of the chain.

“Yes, He is calling me. I will be leaving you soon, little sister.”
The Other's voice came through with that cutting clarity that was all too typical of her.
“He is impatient. He is on his way. He is coming for me.”

Te Atairangikaahu closed her eyes for a second, just long enough to close the report she was no 
longer reading.
- We are going to end this war, she answered. The Blight must be eradicated.
“Let us deliver the deathblow now. This has gone on far too long. You stall.”
There was a new urgency in her tone.
“After that, you will send me across, of your own free will. I wouldn't want to have to compel you, 
little sister.”

A silence. Then, lower, as if whispered right against her ear:
“You owe me at least that much. Without me, you would have choked in their filth.”

She did not answer right away, unable to suppress a shiver.
- You give yourself too much credit, sister. You seem to forget what truly matters. Have you learned
nothing from me?

“Far more than you think, little sister. Far more.”

=== Monde 13 ===
==== Aujourd'hui ====

À bord du Wachablösung, Anepu dormait mal depuis plusieurs nuits. Ses blessures, encore vives, le 
réveillaient par à-coups.

Cette nuit-là, le pendentif qu'il portait autour du cou se fit lourd, puis chaud. La chaleur monta 
jusqu’à devenir brûlante. Une lueur orangée perçait déjà à travers le tissu de sa chemise.
Un réflexe le poussa à porter ses mains à son cou pour arracher le collier, puis, au contact, il 
arrêta son geste.
Il s'assit lentement sur sa couchette, détacha calmement le collier, déposa le coquillage au creux 
de sa paume et ne le quitta plus des yeux.

Lorsqu'il ouvrit enfin la porte de sa cabine pour monter sur le pont, les premiers rayons perçaient 
à l'Est.
Il gagna la proue d'un pas sûr et régulier puis s'accouda au bastingage. Son regard se porta loin 
devant lui, en direction du Nord.
Au creux de sa main, le coquillage roulait lentement entre ses doigts.
