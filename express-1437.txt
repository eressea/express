== A young World - but not so ==

=== The Compass of Æther: Part Three ===

==== The Threshold - Donsor Bay (5) ====

The small boat glided silently into the narrow bay. Its sails snapped one last time before
falling slack. The calm water reflected the rocky cliffs that framed this isolated cove.
Anepu maneuvered cautiously between the outcrops, guided by Grimnarr's nervous instructions.
The keel scraped lightly against the bottom with a dull grinding, then the Tiger Shark came
to rest on the gray pebble beach.
Captain Anepu jumped into the shallow water and pulled the boat toward shore. The sound of
stones rolling beneath his boots echoed in the silence. The bay seemed completely deserted,
yet Anepu knew they were not alone. He sensed the invisible gazes of the Seeleute aus Tidibel.

He waited.

Nothing.

No message from Te Atairangikaahu.

No representative from Tror.

Only silence and the wind blowing softly between the rocks.

Anepu took a deep breath and turned toward the boat.
"Grimnarr," he called softly, "you must come down now. The matriarch has asked that you leave
the boat. You cannot wait inside."

The old goblin, still aboard, stared stubbornly to the southeast, toward the lands beyond the
invisible threshold. His hands trembled slightly on the small boat's rail.
"So much way traveled..." he murmured, his voice thick with regret—and beneath it, a tremor
of fear Anepu could not miss.

Anepu studied the surrounding cliffs for a moment, then turned to them and called out in a
strong voice that echoed against the rock:

"People of Tror, sea folk of Tidibel, I deliver to you the Tiger Shark—a boat that has crossed
worlds and weathered many storms. I thank you for your authorization to pass.
Pilot Grimnarr is with me. He is a master navigator whose knowledge of the Æther Gates is
unmatched. He has guided me faithfully to this place and deserves your respect. I humbly ask
that you treat him with honor."

Grimnarr finally descended, carrying only his old sea bag, his feet touching the pebbles with
a dry sound.
The two travelers stood side by side on the deserted beach, the small boat beached behind
them, waiting in silence for a captain from Tidibel to come and take possession.

Anepu then turned to the old goblin.
He drew from his pockets the folded strip of sailcloth. He opened it, confirming the coins
were all there, then closed it and placed it firmly in Grimnarr's hands.
"This is yours, my friend."

Then he bent down and embraced him—a sincere, deep, and wordless embrace.
Grimnarr, at first surprised, squeezed the captain tightly against him. His shoulders
trembled slightly.

When they separated, Anepu cast a final glance at the Tiger Shark. This small boat had carried
him so far, through so many dangers.
He clutched the shell in his hand, but no contact came. A pang pierced his heart.

He turned abruptly to Grimnarr.
"Come," he said firmly, "we must go toward the village of Sedinkul. Tror's people will surely
communicate their decision about you there."

And they walked away from the beach, their silhouettes gradually diminishing. Behind them,
the solitary Tiger Shark waited on the pebbles, witness to an agreement and pledge of trust.

The sea wind blew across the bay, and somewhere in the heights, a path led to the other side.
