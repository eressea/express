== To all the Peoples of Eressea == 

=== Support for the Appeal to the Elders ===

We, Vers Noirs from the young worlds, support the appeal of Te Arawa!
Like all other young tribes, we struggle to develop in our world, facing
many dangers such as undead, dragons, and other young tribes.
We believe on treaties (at least as far as other tribes are concerned), be
they with stronger tribes or with younger than ourselves.
We believe that diplomacy shall be stronger than shear strength.
We believe that peaceful development has to be preferred to war whenever
possible.
But this has a meaning only if those that shall stand as models for us all
also accept (at least to some extent) this logic. If they don't, war will
wipe us all, and tribes will disappear one after the other until only a
handful of very potent Ancients survive (if they do) and all the worlds
collapse, one after the other, left to the rule of monsters.

**May reason prevail over irrational anger!**


== 10. Welt ==

=== Museum auf Muschelplatau ===

Der erste Raum im Museum nimmt Gestalt an und ich kann euch zwei erste Stücke
aus der Welt Eresseas zeigen. Ein schön behauener Stein wie er in vielen
Gebäuden dieser Welt zum Einsatz kommt. Dieser hier wurde von den Steinhauern
der Teleri mit besonderer Sorgfalt aus dem Fels gemeiselt und mit ihrem Symbol
versehen. Alle sollen sehen, welch herausragende Fähigkeiten sie haben.

Ein Balsam ist das zweite Ausstellungsstück, welches von einem Händler der
Woda'narod für das Museum nach Muschelplateau gesendet wurde. Dieses Gut ist
in so manchen Regionen bei den Bauern sehr begehrt und wird auch zwischen den
Völkern gehandelt.

Ich freue mich natürlich, wenn ihr, werte Völker Eresseas, Baumaterial für das
Museum auf Muschelplateau bringt. Jedes Stück Stein, Eisen oder Holz hilft,
dieses Projekt von der Gemeinschaft für die Gemeinschaft weiter auszubauen.

Bitte denkt daran, eure Vorschläge für den Namen und die Beschreibung des
Museum einzusenden. Es ist noch bis Woche 1435 Zeit dafür. Auch nehme ich gerne
weitere Ideen und Vorschläge für dieses Projekt entgegen.

Euer Perelis
(aus dem Museumsneubau auf Muschelplateau)


== A young World - but not so ==

=== The Compass of Æther: Part Three ===

==== The Threshold - The Approach (1) ====

For all his usual torpor, Grimnarr moved now with a restless energy that surprised Anepu: glancing at the chronocompass again and again, checking lines, rubbing at charts, murmuring old bearings as if the long watch had finally stirred him awake.

Months at sea and weeks since they left the last harbor had forged something quieter than kinship between them: a slow, stubborn friendship born of shared storms, midnight repairs, and the patient counting of notches on the gunwale. At last, Grimnarr seemed to think, their toil and hardship might finally yield what they had risked everything for.

Grimnarr's fingers closed on Anepu's wrist.

"Take the silver," he rasped. "If those desert thugs see my kin with coin, they'll steal—or worse."

He drew forth the leather purse and pressed it into Anepu's hand. "With you beside me, I hope they'll leave me be."

Anepu counted the coins, wrapped them in a strip of sail, and handed the purse back into Grimnarr's care.

"I'll keep them safe," he said. "And I will see that you do not go hungry."

Dawn unfolded over a southern coast of speckled sands. Beyond it the Boundary-Ridges rose, jagged and high—the Gate's threshold shadowed in their crowns.

The chronocompass at Grimnarr's belt hummed, its needle an unerring heartbeat pointing toward the passes and the Æther Gate.

Grimnarr scanned the dunes with concern. Figures must be crouching there, watching: desert thieves whose animosities with his tribe ran old and tangled, favors and debts braided into grudges. He clutched the empty purse like a talisman, his hope for mercy braided with the steadiness of Anepu's presence.

But as they approached the coast, something caught their attention.

Grimnarr straightened abruptly, hand shading his eyes. His other hand rose, pointing toward the horizon with a trembling gesture. His mouth opened, but no words came out. His eyes, usually so keen despite his age, widened with concern.

In the distance, an impressive fleet appeared on the horizon. Ships were approaching at high speed, their sails full, their bows cutting through the waves with determination. 
They were about to bear down on them, crush them without even noticing--a small boat lost in their course.
