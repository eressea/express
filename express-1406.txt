DAMALS

Das ERWACHEN

Dann tauchten Fragen auf.
Was bin ich? Wer bin ich? Wo bin ich?

Als er nach unten blickte, sah er, dass sein Körper grün war.

setzte sich ein Gedanke fest.
"Ich schätzte, dass ich ein - wie hieß das noch mal? - ein Ork bin."
Nachdem die erste Frage beantwortet hatte,
wandte er sich der zweiten Frage zu.
Ein Name kam ihm in den Sinn,
auch wenn er ihm nichts sagte. Gan... Go... Gaurogar.
Das war sein Name.
Immer noch nach unten blickend,
bemerkte er eine kleine Muschel,
die mit einer seltsamen Schnur um seinen Hals gebunden war.

== In the Young World - Somewhere ==

=== THE CALL ===

Barbe Bleue looked around. She was in a study: a table, three chairs, a bed, piles of papers and maps.
But where was she? In what world – and when?
How much time had passed since then?
She knew it instinctively: a long time. A very long time.
She would have to search the memory of the Other.
Her name? She searched softly, furtively.
Te Atairangikaahu, Te Awara clan.
Her world?
It was blocked.
She stopped. She felt danger – personally dangerous.

Before taking any risks, she had to contact Gaurogar.
Was he still wearing the shell? What about the Other’s shell?
She reached for her neck — nothing.
She rummaged through drawers and cupboards until she found it in a small pearly box: The Other’s Birth SeaShell.
She slipped it around her neck.
Now she had to harmonize her mental waves with those of the shell.
She emptied her mind.
Period, wavelength, frequency.
And then she was in tune.

Now it was time to create the reflection — the place, the Shadow, the alternate reality.
Extreme focus. Memory recall. Summoning of smells, sounds, sensations.
(Image)
Children running and tumbling in the grass.
(Now, add colors)
An azure sky, a yolk-yellow sun.
(Add sounds)
Shrieks and laughter echoing.
(Add smells)
Freshly cut grass, wood smoke, damp earth after rain.
(Add touch)
The soft fabric of her trousers, a few coins in her pockets.
(Add emotion)
Well-being after rest, fulfillment, completeness.

Now it was time to see him.
A risky gamble, since the Other was lurking in her own flesh, ready to expel her.
But she had to lower her guard, open her mind, spread her senses, focus her entire being on a single goal.

(Image)
Children are running. I’m sitting on the grass, my back against an oak
:he is there
(Hearing) I hear him breathing beside me, his deep, calm, grounded voice, echoing within me like a steady chant - and calming me
:nearby, sitting against the same tree
(Smell) his familiar scent fills my being, soothes and reassures me
:I feel his presence beside me
(Touch) the warmth of his body radiates. His tangible aura merges with mine. His arm rests against mine
:sends a shiver through me -
(Taste) and the salty taste of his skin floods my mouth
:I turn my head, open my eyes, and see his face
(Sight) his piercing hazel eyes, and that gaze where hardness meets a tenderness tinged with sorrow
The image sharpens, deepens, draws breath
The shell is there, around his neck, glistening softly
I reach out to touch it.
(Find the way to him)

I channel EVERYTHING — my soul, my essence, my spirit - distilled into a single pulse of will

RIGHT NOW

I dive into the shell

I SHOUT, I CRY OUT his NAME -

G A U R O G A R

The energy erupts, leaping from shell to shell, spreading forward
Each shell relays the signal to the next, in a wild race, toward the final target.

ping...

Shattered, Barbe Bleue collapses, exhausted.
Images flash before her eyes
:children running and tumbling in the grass
 ...children   ..... My Children      ....OUR CHILDREN.

She passes out.
