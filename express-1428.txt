== 10. Welt ==

=== Museum auf Muschelplateau ===

Muschelplateau ist seit jeher ein heiliger Ort des Friedens, der Diplomatie und
des kulturellen Austauschs – ein Ort, der allen Völkern Eresseas offen steht.
Seit 20 Jahren ist es unberührt von Konflikten und war ein Ort der Besonnenheit,
wenn es außen keine Besonnenheit mehr gab. Ein Ort, der auch für den Austausch
von Wissen stand und steht. Dazu hat auch das Museum im Crytalturm beigetragen.

Wir werden auf Muschelplateau ein neues Museumsgebäude errichten, das diese 
Bestimmung noch klarer verkörpert.

Vision des neuen Museums:
Dieses Museum wird wahrhaft allen Völkern gehören, als gemeinsames Kulturgut
Eresseas.

Jede Nation ist eingeladen:
An der Gestaltung und Verwaltung teilzunehmen.
Gegenstände auszustellen, die ihre Geschichte und Kultur repräsentieren.
Das Museum zu besuchen und zu genießen.

Perelis wird das neue Gebäude einrichten und die ersten Ausstellungsstücke organisieren. Ich selbst werde erneut Stücke aus meiner Privatsammlung beisteuern.
Dazu kommen erneut Spenden anderer Völker, unter anderem von der UN, welche
ihren Platz im Museum finden.

Wir rufen alle Völker dazu auf, Vorschläge für die Benennung und Beschreibung 
dieses Gebäudes einzusenden.
Bitte sendet Eure Ideen an den Taubenschlag der XE-Redaktion bis Woche 1435.
Jeder Vorschlag wird geprüft und berücksichtigt. 

Woschak
für ein sicheres und gemeinschaftliches Muschelplateau

== A young World - but not so ==

=== The Compass of Æther: Part Two (3) ===

==== The Long Route ====

Grimnarr was not finished. He still scrutinized the chronocompass, brow furrowed, as if reading something written in minuscule letters.

"After two weeks eastward," he resumed in a slower, thinner voice, "you will turn to a course of southeast for three further weeks."

Anepu counted mentally. Eight weeks total since their departure. Nearly three full months to reach this Æther Gate.

"There," the goblin continued, and his voice took on an almost dreamy tone, "the water will take on the shimmer of another realm. You will see it. It is not something that can be mistaken. The ocean itself changes color, as if... as if two worlds were mingling in the waves."

He passed a trembling hand over his face.

"When the chronocompass's needle wavers between two directions, you will know the Gate is close. At that moment, reduce sail and listen."

"What should I hear?" asked Anepu.

"Its song," Grimnarr answered simply. "A low, humming vibration that travels just beneath the surface. Like... like the heartbeat of the world itself. You will hear it. I am certain of it. A water reader like you cannot miss it."

The goblin swayed slightly.

"Remind me then," he murmured. "When you hear the song. I will have to perform the final calculations..."

Then Grimnarr slid against his crates and his eyes closed. Anepu was about to ask him for more details about the timing, but the old scholar was already snoring.

The captain straightened and contemplated the eastern horizon.

Five more weeks of navigation before reaching the Æther Gate. And then what?

He glanced at his provisions. They would hold.

He sniffed the wind, which blew good and steady from the west, adjusted the sails, and came about.

The Tiger Shark obeyed with grace, her bow turning eastward, leaving behind the enchanting forest of Wildshire.

Ahead, the ocean stretched on, and somewhere a Gate awaited him.

A Gate between worlds.

=== Bloody Heavy Cross ===

==== Ile de TAEH ====

===== Marae de Te Henga =====

It had begun several weeks ago.
An entire season of withheld blood. Of cold decisions. Of sleepless nights where ink dried on her fingers.
The tribes kept coming, bearing their oaths, their weapons, their hopes — and she accepted them all.
Every fleet bore her name carved into the prow. Every raised sail was a seal she had drawn.
The entire machinery slid forward under her command.
The Rite of Reckoning brought each death back to her door.
Without her, nothing advanced.
With her, everything advanced too fast — she could not stop it.

She watched the maps, the numbers.
Her sisters were the first to see, the first to fall — and the first to strike.

The world was tipping. And she with it.

She buried her doubts beneath dispatches.
She crushed her fatigue under Discipline.
She suffocated herself beneath Duty and Responsibility.

For some time now, the signs had returned. Subtle. Persistent. 
That metallic taste filling her mouth, the sudden quickening of her pulse, that heat rising from the base of her spine.
Sensations she knew, had tried to ignore — but since that day, ignored no longer.

She forced herself to look at the missives.
A new fleet had to depart before dawn. A supply convoy needed to be rerouted.
One last line to draw, and the conflict would shift in scale.
She took up the stylus.

“No.”

The word fell — naked as a guilty confession.

In that inner void, something stirred, a breath, a presence.
A voice.

— I am the decision you refuse to make.

The voice was warm like a hearth, smooth and soft like velvet, sliding into her thoughts like a boa.

— You never truly had a choice.

The heat escalated to a blaze, running up her spine like a searing blade.

Te Atairangikaahu drew a silent breath, turned her inner gaze, and recited:
  I will not face obliteration…
  I will let intrusion reveal me…
  It will pass through me…
  And where it fails, only I will remain, indivisible…

— You recite. You breathe. But you tremble, Te Atairangikaahu.

Fatigue made her concentration waver for an instant.

— You are wasting this body with your principles. Give it to me for one night. Just one. I will make it roar.

A surge of instinct — burning, feral, starving — overwhelmed her.

— I am the inescapable.
— I am the merciless.
— I am the edge of your blade.

Te Atairangikaahu did not respond.

— I am you as much as you are me.

Her breathing grew even slower. Rhythmic, controlled, perfect.

— We are Her hand.

*Steady Growth, Shared Prosperity: FORTUNE’s Continued Expansion*

The halflings of *FORTUNE* are now more numerous than in the early days,
and their work is gradually extending into new regions. Every territory
where they operate sees its resources better managed, its infrastructure
strengthened, and its economic activity revived or enhanced.

Quietly and without pretension, FORTUNE fulfills the purpose for which it
was created:
*to develop local wealth for the exclusive benefit of the peoples and
leaders who welcome us.*

Everywhere FORTUNE works, the effects are already noticeable:
improved circulation of goods, optimized production, steady logistical
support… all essential foundations for lasting prosperity.

And yet, this is only the beginning.

The speed of development depends above all on the trust that governments
place in FORTUNE.
*The stronger that trust becomes, the faster projects can expand,
accelerate, and bear fruit.*

For wherever leaders open the path,
*the halflings of FORTUNE follow — and the population thrives.*
