== A young World - but not so ==

=== The Compass of Æther: Part Three ===

==== The Threshold - A New World (7) ====

They stood at the summit of the pass, contemplating the panorama that stretched below.
The time had come for their descent.

But before beginning the descent, Anepu suddenly felt the shell at his neck grow
warm against his chest.

Te Atairangikaahu.

At last.

The matriarch's voice resonated in his mind:

*Captain. I have received the response from Tror, First Captain of the Seeleute aus
Tidibel.*

Anepu's heart began to beat faster.

*Walk two weeks to the southeast. There, a boat will wait for you. It is damaged,
but it can sail. And Grimnarr is authorized to accompany you. Safe journey.*

Relief washed over Anepu.
He opened his eyes and turned to Grimnarr, who watched him, waiting.

"The Seeleute have accepted," Anepu said simply.
"They have agreed that you may continue the voyage. And they have granted us a
boat."

Grimnarr remained motionless for a moment, then nodded slowly.
His lips trembled slightly, but he said nothing.
No words were needed.

Anepu saw the tension leave the old goblin's shoulders, the terrible weight of
uncertainty finally lifting.

"Let us go," Grimnarr said in a hoarse voice.
"Our boat awaits us."

They descended slowly toward the bays, scanning every corner, following a narrow
path that wound between the rocks.
Each step brought them closer to the new world, each turn revealing more of the
panorama that stretched below.

Rounding a rocky promontory, they finally glimpsed them.

Men waited near a small boat beached on the pebbles.
Even from afar, Anepu recognized Tidibel's colors on their clothing.
The boat—the Lichtstrahl XXI, no doubt—rested on its side, visibly damaged but
seaworthy.

Immense relief flooded Anepu.
They had kept their word.
The boat was there.

But as he was about to quicken his pace, something caught his attention.

Not far off, on a rocky promontory overlooking the cove, a group of dark
silhouettes crouched.
Ghasts.
They feasted on their latest meal, tearing strips of flesh with jerky,
ravenous movements.

In the morning light, fragments of greenish flesh could still be seen clinging
to bones.
A goblin.
Yet another dead goblin, devoured.

Grimnarr stopped dead.

His body froze completely, as if his legs suddenly refused to carry him further.
His eyes were fixed on the promontory, on the creatures feasting, on the remains
of one who could have been him.

His face had taken on a grayish tint.

Anepu gently placed his hand on the goblin's shoulder.

"Come," he said in a firm but gentle voice.

They approached the Tidibel men discreetly, staying close to the rocks to remain
out of sight of the predators.

When they reached the group, Anepu stepped forward and spoke in a clear but
measured voice:

"Men of Tror, Master Pilot Grimnarr the Rift-Warder of the Taggräber clan, and I,
Anepu Kerehonone of the Te Arawa clan, greet you.
You are here as promised, despite the dangers and risks of these lands.
We are deeply grateful to you."

He cast a glance at the boat beached on the shore.

"May we take possession of the Lichtstrahl XXI this week?"

"And do you have sailing orders for me, showing the route to follow and the
instructions to observe?"

Grimnarr stood slightly back, his eyes still carefully avoiding the promontory
where the ghasts continued their macabre feast.

Anepu concluded solemnly:

"It will be said in all the worlds we cross that the Seeleute aus Tidibel are men
of honor and of their word."

The sea wind blew across the bay, bringing a scent of iodine mingled with the
intoxicating fragrance of discovery.

Before them, the Lichtstrahl XXI waited.

And beyond, the ancient worlds were calling.

== Unknown World ==

=== The Turning of the Bones ===

"No, don't tell me," said the Chieftan, staring down at the cowering peasant. "Let me guess. More Mumma [undead]? Every week someone brings me fresh news of them rising from their graves."

His voice was grim.

"Nobody to' me 'bout this when they asked me to lead 'em. C'rana, they promised. Norsca. Puushanni! Taarka'nu, even! Gibbaz! Nobody even *mentioned* 'problems'," he thought to himself, bitterly.

"M-m-my Lord..."

The goblin quivered, and passed a hand from his brow down across his face indicating his shame at having to bring this news before the Chieftan.

"Where this time?" the Chieftan asked, in an exasperated tone.

"..."

"Oh for the gods' sakes, man! Just POINT!"

The peasant slowly raised a trembling arm and pointed off to his left.

"For f... AT THE MAP!" bellowed the Chieftan, gesturing towards a map that was being held up by a servant standing a couple of paces away. "AT THE..." he shook his head in despair.

"Truly, the gods have forsaken me, for I am surrounded by khaz'lid..." he added in a low voice, as had no doubt so many leaders before him.

"H-here, my L-lord," said the peasant, pointing towards an area of the map.

The Chieftan nodded.

"Finally," the Chieftan sighed. Aloud, he said "Very well. We will gather warriors and march to battle as soon as we can. We will free the people," he reassured the peasant, and all others in earshot, realising that they too needed such.

"Or we will add to the undead numbers ourselves," he added to himself in his head.


Cryphka, scribe for Ur Kazvar Seti'Do of the Chæn people
