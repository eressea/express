== 10. Welt ==

=== Bauarbeiten begonnen ===

In den frühen Morgenstunden wurden einige Botschafter und Besucher auf 
Muschelplateau durch laute Geräusche geweckt. In der Nähe der Taverne waren eine
Menge Meermenschen in Arbeitskleidung zu sehen, die eifrig Baumaterialien trugen
und an einem neuen monumentalen Gebäude arbeiteten. Vor der Baustellenabsperrung 
stand ein Schild:

 Museumsneubau
 
 Gemeinsam bauen wir hier das Eressea-Museum und gestalten die Zukunft neu.
 
Man sah auch Perelis, wie er mit dem Bauleiter sprach. Er wollte gerne einige
seiner Vorstellungen zum Neubau einbringen, wurde jedoch überstimmt, da vorrangig
die Vorschläge anderer Völker umgesetzt werden.

Die Bauleitung äußerte erneut den Wunsch, dass gerne weitere Vorschläge für den
Namen des Gebäudes sowie die Beschreibung eingereicht werden können.


== A young World - but not so ==

=== The Compass of Æther: Part Two (4) ===

==== The Storm and the Trance ====

The Tiger Shark had just weathered a storm. Anepu Kerehonone had managed to avoid the worst of the swell: the ship had groaned, creaked, but held fast. Only the steering sail had torn as he struggled to maintain course.

He looked around, assessing the additional damage. A few lines to replace, nothing catastrophic.

But something had changed. Each breath seemed to crackle on his tongue.

Anepu rushed toward the crates where the goblin was huddled, still gripping his instruments.

"Master Pilot Grimnarr? Are you not injured?" The goblin was huddled among his crates, but unharmed. "The tempest caught us by surprise as we approached the Gate..." He hesitated, then added: "The ship hasn't suffered major damage, but the chronocompass is acting up. Is it time for your learned calculations?"

He had wanted to appear calm and composed, but his unease showed through nonetheless. However experienced his guest might be, he didn't know how the goblin would react to such an ordeal. And, to be frank, he had feared for a moment losing his passenger—a shame for a captain of his lineage.

While Grimnarr gathered his belongings, Anepu set about repairing the small sail. The air was heavy and vibrant. A throbbing hum rose from the surface, like the snarl of a feline crouched beneath the waves.

Without looking up from his work, he continued in a lower voice: "This passage between worlds... is it here?"

A silence followed.

Then, in an undertone: "Shall we cross it together?"

Finally, he raised his gaze toward the goblin: "Or must our matriarch make new arrangements for the remainder of the voyage right now?"

Grimnarr opened one eye, then the other, the chronocompass cupped in his palm like something fragile and secret. The instrument's crystal caught the pale sunlight and scattered it into a dozen tiny points that danced across his weathered fingers. He let the device rest against his chest and drew a breath that rattled from tired lungs.

"I could imagine us riding the Æther Gate together—you and I, it is good company and worthwhile talking about foregone adventures in other worlds," he said, slow and small. The words came out softer than Anepu had heard before—less boast, more possibility.

Grimnarr turned the compass until the needle trembled; it spun once, then quivered between two faintly luminous pins.

"But not yet. Not without proper measure."

He set the chronocompass on the crate and tapped the glass with a practiced fingertip.

"This tool does not merely point," he explained, voice thinning with fatigue. "It tastes the seams of time. I must coax it—feed it moments, hours folded into pattern—until its song grows steady. That means long readings. Two weeks, at least. While it draws, I fall inward. It is not sleep like yours; it is a trance that peels me from this hour and sinks me into the instrument's listening. Do not ring bells. Do not shout my name. Leave a candle and a small bowl of salt; if the trance fractures, the salt steadies the count."

Anepu's face tightened. The captain had already patched the torn steering sail and kept the Tiger Shark from broaching in the last swell; now his passenger asked for something that sounded like abandoning the wheel.

Grimnarr's hands trembled when he gathered his charts; each movement was measured, practiced—an old ritual performed despite the goblin's exhaustion.

"It will not be in vain," Grimnarr added, answering the look in Anepu's eyes. "If the needle quivers toward the Gate, wake me gently. If it remains dull, let it drink longer. Time will coax truth from it."

Anepu swallowed. He pictured two weeks of quiet, the ship gliding more slowly than even other races could sail—let alone seaborn men of his kin. It felt like a gamble, but the alternative—rushing an uncertain passage—felt worse.

He found his voice steadier than he expected. "Very well. We keep course. No bells. I will mark the hours myself."

Grimnarr nodded once, eyelids heavy. He reached for a length of braided twine and tied it once around the chronocompass's base, a small seal of habit as much as precaution.

Then the goblin eased back among his crates, the trance already pulling at the edges of his awareness. His breathing slowed, his lips moved in soft, counting tones, and the compass's glow warmed like a small hearth.

The silent vigil had begun.

=== Für Die Königin in ihrem Garten ===

Eines Morgens lief die Goblinkönigin durch ihren Blumengarten. 
Plötzlich blieb sie stehen: Auf ihrem Lieblingsfelsen lag ein seltsames kleines Paket. 
Darauf stand in schöner Schrift: „Dein Aufruf hat mich berührt.“ 
Als sie es öffnete, schoss ein kleiner Regenbogen heraus und ließ für einen Moment ihre Quietschblumen und die schiefe Krone glänzen. 
Sobald das Licht verflogen war, entdeckte sie 7 Mützen, ordentlich aufgereiht, in den 7 Farben des Regenbogens.
